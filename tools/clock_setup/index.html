Time & timezone

— : — : —Not connected

Both boards: press HOUR or MINUTE once to advance; hold to repeat. Buttons reset seconds. On R4 WiFi, manual setting pauses NTP until restart or Resume NTP. Without NTP or an RTC, set time again after power loss or reset.

03

Wi-Fi R4 WiFi

Wi-Fi settings are available after connecting an R4 WiFi.

Use a 2.4 GHz network. Credentials are sent over USB and stored on the Arduino; they are never saved by this tool. NTP syncs at startup and hourly while connected.

04

Meter calibration

Hours use a 0–15 V voltmeter. Minutes require a 0–100 µA movement and a calibrated 0–60 dial. The present board supplies about 73 µA maximum. Enable only after wiring and calibration have been checked.
